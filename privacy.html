Privacy

At Navlakha Technologies, we treat your data just like we treat our own: responsibly. The best testament to this commitment is our track record — every business we serve, we serve with the same promise.

Privacy has a lot to do with choosing a business model. We chose one that is not in conflict with protecting your information. Our business model is simple: we offer powerful software at fair prices. When you find value in what we offer, you pay us for it. We have no hidden agendas, no back-end deals with advertisers.

01 — Ownership

Your data is yours. Always.

You're the owner — we're the custodian. Export it, port it, delete it. No lock-in, no asterisks.

02 — No Trade

Not for sale. Not now, not ever.

Your records are never packaged, brokered, or shared with third parties for profit. That's a hard rule.

03 — Ad-Free

Zero ads. Zero ad tracking.

Our software is paid for by the businesses that use it — not by advertisers. So we've no reason to watch you.
