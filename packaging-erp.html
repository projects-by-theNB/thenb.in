FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Packaging ERP.

  • What does the GSM structure engine do?

    You enter each layer's density and microns; GSM (density × microns) and each layer's share of the finished weight are calculated automatically. That one structure then drives the job card, material planning, purchasing and costing.

  • Can the job card show the artwork?

    Yes. Artwork and cylinder proofs are attached to the item by reference and printed on the job card, with a mirror view for reverse-printed jobs, so the press sees exactly what the customer approved.

  • How does reel-level inventory work?

    Every reel gets a unique number with width, micron, gross and net weight, supplier lot and GRN. Reels are issued against specific work orders, partly consumed reels return with the correct balance, and remnants stay visible and re-usable.

  • How is Packaging ERP rolled out?

    In four phases: the technical core (item, artwork, cylinder, structure engine, job card) goes live first, followed by materials & stores, shop-floor capture, and finally estimation, costing, QC, dispatch and dashboards — each phase built on the system your team already uses.

  • Can it calculate cost per 1,000 pouches?

    Yes. Cost per kg, per metre and per 1,000 pouches is derived from the actual structure, current film rates, ink and adhesive consumption, cylinder amortisation and conversion cost by machine hour.
