FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might wonder about Connect.

  • Is Connect really free?

    Yes. Connect is completely free to download and use. There is no sign-up, no subscription, and no paywall on the relationship features. We built it as a free, public tool for anyone who wants to stay close to the people who matter.

  • Does Connect upload my contacts or call history?

    No. Connect is local-first. All analysis of your contacts and call log happens on your device, and that data is only ever stored on your phone. Connect never syncs your personal relationship data to any server — there is nothing to leak because nothing leaves your device.

  • Do I need an account to use Connect?

    No account is required. Connect is the default experience when you open the app without logging in. Just grant contacts and call-log access and tap Analyse — that's it.

  • How does Connect decide who I should reconnect with?

    Connect looks at your real call patterns — how often you used to talk, how long it has been since your last call, missed calls, and how strong the relationship once was — using a transparent, interpretable formula (no black-box AI). It then surfaces people you were once close to but have drifted from, with a plain-language reason for each.

  • Which platforms is Connect available on?

    Connect is available on both Android and iOS. Download it free from the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store — you get the same reconnect dashboard, groups, notes, and on-device privacy on either platform.
