Delete your account & data

This page explains how to request deletion of your Navlakha Technologies app account, or of specific data, and tells you exactly what is deleted, what is retained, and for how long. It applies to all apps we publish.

Developer: Navlakha Technologies · Applies to all Navlakha Technologies apps · Last updated: 31 August 2026

1. How to have your account deleted

Our apps are business tools. In most cases your account is created and owned by the organisation that issued it to you — your employer or the business you work with — and only your organisation's administrator can remove it.

  1. In the app: if your app offers it, open your profile or account menu and choose Delete Account & Data, then confirm. You are signed out immediately.
  2. Through your administrator: ask your organisation's administrator to delete your account from the app's admin workspace.

Deleting the account removes your profile and the personal data listed in section 3, on the schedule in that section.

Your organisation is unreachable, or you no longer work with them? Email support@navlakha.tech with the subject “Delete my account”, the app name, and the phone number or email you registered with. We will verify the request with the organisation that holds the account and action it.

2. Request deletion of specific data (without deleting your account)

If you want to remove certain data but keep your account active, you can request deletion of specific data only:

  1. Send an email to support@navlakha.tech with the subject line “Delete my data”.
  2. Tell us which app and which data you want removed, and include the phone number or email you registered with.
  3. We verify the request, delete the specified data, and email you a confirmation. Your account remains active.

3. What data is deleted and what is retained

Data On account deletion
Account & profile (name, registered phone number or email, login credentials)Deleted
Work records you created or were assigned in the app (for example contacts, attendance, visits, call history, documents)Deleted
Reminders, schedules and notesDeleted
App and device telemetry (app version, sync status)Deleted or anonymized
Records we are legally required to keep (e.g. for tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention)Retained for the period required by law, then deleted

Retention periods

  • Active systems: your account and personal data are deleted within 30 days of a verified request.
  • Backups: data may persist in encrypted, access-restricted backups for up to 90 days after deletion, after which it is permanently purged.
  • Legal retention: a limited set of records may be retained only where required by applicable law, and is deleted once that obligation ends.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your data is deleted it cannot be recovered.
