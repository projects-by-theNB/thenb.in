FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about College & University ERP.

  • Is Campus ERP only for colleges and universities?

    It is built for colleges and universities, and works equally for schools and other institutions. Credit-based NEP 2020 structures, SGPA / CGPA and university-style examinations are supported alongside CBSE, ICSE, state-board and CCE grade cards.

  • Does it include a full Learning Management System?

    Yes. Every subject in the timetable becomes a course with structured content, live classes through Zoom, Google Meet or Teams, assignments, online tests and a gradebook — and teachers can see which students actually opened the material.

  • Can we use our existing biometric machines for attendance?

    Yes. Campus ERP works with eSSL, ZKTeco, Biomax, Suprema, Matrix and Identix devices, plus face recognition, RFID cards and a teacher mobile app — so there is no vendor lock-in.

  • Does it help with NAAC, NBA, AISHE and UDISE+ reporting?

    Yes. Enrolment, attendance, staff, results, infrastructure and asset data are already in the system and can be exported in the formats those submissions ask for.

  • Is staff payroll included?

    Yes. Teaching and non-teaching payroll runs on pay scales and grade pay, with arrears, increments, PF, ESIC, PT, TDS and Form 16 automated, and payslips delivered on WhatsApp.
