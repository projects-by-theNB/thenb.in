The Problem

Where a Food Plant's Margin Goes

Measured every day, not at month-end.

Packing Over-Fill

Free product in every packet. Machine-wise weight sampling, tare corrected, gives a daily Give-Away % per SKU.

Oven & Packing Broken

Rejects never measured. Oven broken and packing broken tracked separately, machine by machine.

Floor Sweepage

Maida, sugar and dough lost daily. Sweepage by ingredient and section — mixing vs packing.

Wrapper & Lamination

BOPP and inner-roll waste on changeovers. Consumption vs wastage % per SKU per day.

Energy Blind Spot

One bill a month, no idea which line burned it. Electricity, gas, solar and DG per tonne — plant-wise and shift-wise.

Missed Dispatch Targets

Penalties found at month-end. Daily party-wise target vs actual, with the monthly SKU balance live.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about FMCG & Food Factory ERP.

  • Which FMCG plants is this built for?

    It is built for food and FMCG manufacturing plants — biscuits, bakery and similar packaged-food lines — where give-away, broken product, sweepage and wrapper wastage decide the margin. The full ERP (purchase, stores, sales, QC, assets) is included, so it also fits other FMCG production setups.

  • How is give-away calculated?

    Packets are weight-sampled on up to 16 machines with wrapper tare corrected. Give-Away % = (Actual − Standard) ÷ Standard, reported daily per SKU.

  • Can we track electricity and gas per tonne?

    Yes. Grid, solar, DG and diesel units and gas meter readings are recorded per plant and shift, and divided by real production output to give energy per tonne that is comparable across lines and shifts.

  • Does it support CAPA for customer complaints?

    Yes. Each CAPA records source, batch, lot, quantity rejected, root cause, corrective action, owner, timeline and evidence photos, with a repeat-complaint flag and a printable audit report.

  • How quickly can we go live?

    Masters and targets load from Excel and supervisors can start entering data from day one, on shop-floor tablets — most plants are live in weeks.
