Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Navlakha Technologies apps collect, how it is used, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you delete it. It applies to all apps we publish. Individual apps request only the permissions and data they need for their features, so not every item below applies to every app.

Developer: Navlakha Technologies · Applies to all Navlakha Technologies apps · Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who controls your data

Our apps are business tools. In most cases you do not sign up for them yourself — your employer or the organisation you work with subscribes to the app and issues you an account.

Your organisation is the data controller. It decides what work data is put into the app and who inside the organisation can see it. Navlakha Technologies operates the app and servers as the data processor, acting on your organisation's instructions under our service agreement with them.

If you want to know how your employer uses the data, or you want to object to it, speak to them first. We can act only within what our agreement with them allows, except where the law gives you rights directly against us.

2. What we collect and why

Depending on the app and the features your organisation uses, we may collect:

DataWhy we collect it
Account & profile — your name, username, phone number, email if provided, and an internal user ID To sign you in, link your work to your account, and let your organisation's administrators manage their team.
Work records — the business data you create or are assigned in the app (for example contacts, attendance, visits, tasks, documents or transactions) These are your organisation's business records and power the app's core features for you and your managers.
Location — GPS coordinates, including in the background, in apps that offer field tracking To log GPS-verified field visits and route or shift attendance. Background location runs only while you have started a shift or task and stops when you end it.
Camera & photos — taken with the camera, or chosen from your library, in apps that use them Verification (for example shift start/end or a site visit) and evidence attached to a record you create.
Device contacts / call data — only in apps whose purpose requires it, and only with your separate consent To match a saved name to a number or keep a work call history against the right customer. We never read or upload your address book in bulk.
Reminders, schedules and notes To bring follow-ups and tasks back to you at the time you chose.
App and device telemetry — app version, sync status, basic device and OS information To keep sync working, support you when something breaks, and prompt you to update.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Our apps contain no advertising SDK and no cross-app tracking.

3. Device permissions

Every permission an app requests is optional at the operating-system level and you can withdraw any of them at any time in your device settings. Withdrawing one disables the feature that needs it; the rest of the app keeps working. Apps request only the permissions their features need, which may include location (including “Always” for shift tracking), camera and photos, contacts, and notifications.

4. Who your data is shared with

  • Your organisation. Authorised administrators and managers in your organisation can see the work data you record. This is the purpose of the product.
  • Service providers we rely on — for example map / geocoding services used to turn coordinates into a readable address. Only the minimum data needed is sent, and no more personal data than necessary is included in such requests.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers, who store and serve the data on our behalf under contracts that require them to protect it to the standard set out in this policy.

Any third party we share data with is required, by contract, to provide protection for your data equal to that described in this policy and to use it only for the purpose we gave it to them. We do not share your data with advertising networks, analytics providers, data brokers, or AI services. We may disclose data where we are legally required to.

5. How we protect it

All traffic between the app and our servers uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to production data is restricted to authorised personnel. Backups are encrypted and access-restricted.

6. How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active and your organisation subscribes to the app. You can delete your account at any time — full detail, including exactly what is deleted and what is retained, is on the account & data deletion page.

Retention periods

  • Active systems: your account and personal data are deleted within 30 days of a verified request.
  • Backups: data may persist in encrypted, access-restricted backups for up to 90 days after deletion, then is permanently purged.
  • Legal retention: records your organisation must keep by law — for example attendance and payroll history needed for tax and accounting — are retained for the period the law requires, then deleted. Those records belong to your organisation, not to your individual login.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how it is processed, or withdraw consent you previously gave. To exercise any of these, email support@navlakha.tech with the subject “Privacy request”, the app name, and the phone number or email you registered with, so we can locate and verify your account.

Because your organisation is the data controller, we may need to pass your request to them, or ask them to authorise it, before we can act on it. We will tell you if that happens.

8. Children

Our apps are workplace tools issued to employees. They are not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child's data has reached us, email us and we will delete it. See also our Child Safety Standards.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting how your data is used will also be communicated to your organisation.
