Child Safety Standards & CSAE Policy

Navlakha Technologies maintains a strict, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). This page sets out our published standards prohibiting child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any conduct that endangers children. These standards apply to every app we publish, in line with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

Developer: Navlakha Technologies · Applies to all Navlakha Technologies apps · Last updated: 23 June 2026

Zero tolerance. Navlakha Technologies strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form. We do not permit, tolerate, or facilitate any content or behaviour that sexualizes, abuses, exploits, or endangers a minor anywhere in our apps or services.

1. Who we are & scope

Navlakha Technologies develops and publishes business and productivity applications. These child safety standards apply to all of our apps and to all users, content, and interactions within them. Our apps are business tools and are not intended for use by children.

2. Prohibited conduct (CSAE)

The following are strictly prohibited in any Navlakha Technologies app and will result in immediate removal, account termination, and reporting to the relevant authorities:

  • Creating, uploading, storing, sharing, requesting, or linking to child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any content that sexualizes minors.
  • Grooming, soliciting, or attempting to sexually exploit, abuse, or endanger a child.
  • Sexual extortion (sextortion), trafficking, or any sexualization of a minor.
  • Promoting, normalizing, or facilitating any of the above, including sharing contact details or external links for these purposes.

3. Our standards & safeguards

  • We comply with all applicable child safety laws and with Google Play's Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Safety Standards policies.
  • We provide an email mechanism (see below) for users to report suspected CSAE, and we review every report.
  • Confirmed CSAE content is removed, the responsible account is terminated, and the matter is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or local law enforcement as required.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement and child-safety authorities in investigating CSAE.

4. How to report a child safety concern

If you encounter any content or behaviour in one of our apps that may involve the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child, report it to us immediately:

  1. Email support@navlakha.tech with the subject line “Child Safety / CSAE report”.
  2. Tell us which app, describe the content or behaviour, where you encountered it, and any details that help us locate and act on it.
  3. We review and act on child safety reports as a priority and report confirmed CSAE to the appropriate authorities.
Report a child safety concern

In an emergency where a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services right away. In India you can also reach the national child helpline on 1098. Suspected CSAM can be reported to the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org.

5. Designated child safety contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety and CSAE matters is the Navlakha Technologies support team at support@navlakha.tech.
