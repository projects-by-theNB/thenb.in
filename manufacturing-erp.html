FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Manufacturing ERP.

  • Is this different from the Manufacturing module in your ERP Suite?

    Manufacturing ERP is a complete, factory-focused package: it bundles BOMs and work orders with stores and purchase, three QC gates, Plant Life Cycle Management (preventive and breakdown maintenance) and costing & traceability — configured for how a plant actually runs.

  • Does it handle both preventive and breakdown maintenance?

    Yes. Preventive maintenance is scheduled by calendar, running hours or production counter, with checklists and auto-generated PM work orders. Breakdowns are raised from the floor by mobile or QR scan, with technician assignment, SLA, a downtime clock, spares from store and root cause on closure.

  • Will I know the true cost of each unit?

    Yes. Job-card labour time, material consumption and overhead roll up to a true cost per unit per work order, with production-vs-planned variance and scrap captured at every stage.

  • Can we migrate from our existing system?

    Yes. Masters, BOMs and machines are imported from Excel, including column mapping from legacy ERPs, so most plants go live in weeks rather than months.

  • How is pricing decided?

    Pricing is quoted based on the number of users, plants and the data to be migrated. Book a callback and we will set up one of your BOMs and machines live.
