Frequently asked

I can't log in to the app. What should I do?

First check your internet connection and that you're using the credentials provided by your organisation's administrator. If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot password" option on the login screen, or ask your administrator to reset it. Still stuck? Email us the registered mobile number or email and we'll help.

How do I get an account or add users?

Accounts are set up by your organisation's administrator. If you're setting up for your business for the first time, contact us using the form or the details above and we'll guide you through onboarding.

How is my data handled?

We take data protection seriously. See our Privacy Policy for details on what we collect, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

How do I delete my account or data?

See our account & data deletion page for how to delete your account or specific data, what's removed, and our retention periods.

I found a bug or the app is behaving unexpectedly.

Please let us know using the form, including your device model, OS version, and the steps that led to the issue (a screenshot or screen recording helps a lot). We'll investigate and get back to you.

How do I request a new feature?

We'd love to hear it. Send your idea through the form or email — feedback from users directly shapes what we build next.

How do I report a child safety concern?

See our Child Safety Standards & CSAE policy for how to report a concern. We review every report as a priority.
